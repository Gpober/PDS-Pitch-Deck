Risk Assessment & Mitigation

1. The $2.82M Note — Gating Risk

Risk: The $2,821,500 convertible note was assumed by the former partner in the 2025 transition. Without an executed novation (or subordination plus full standby), the note would be treated as a Company obligation and the SBA facility does not fund — global coverage would fall to approximately 0.52×.

Mitigation: Executed documentation is the #1 item on the diligence checklist and a condition precedent to the expansion plan. $15K of this raise is allocated to the legal work. Investors should treat SBA close as conditional on this document.

2. Customer Concentration

Risk: Manheim (Cox) and Dent Wizard represent 99.7% of 2026 revenue, up from 63.7% in 2025 — and a single site, Manheim Dallas, is 54.5% on its own. Non-anchor revenue has fallen to $2,905. Accounts including South Florida Auction ($541,493 in 2025), Manheim Statesville ($411,204), North Fort Lauderdale Subaru ($222,246), Sawgrass Infiniti ($159,369) and Music City Auto Auction ($139,513) did not carry into 2026.

Mitigation: $25K of this raise funds a sales and diversification rebuild across independent auctions, fleet, and OEM channels; the $1.5M expansion adds a new auction-site location that widens the base. Remaining relationships are multi-year, multi-location, and anchored by Supplier Diversity Program certification.

3. Goodwill Dominates the Balance Sheet

Risk: $3,633,969 of the $3,776,646 asset base is goodwill from the 2025 buyout. Tangible asset coverage is thin: $76,854 of cash and $59,323 of receivables at year-end 2025.

Mitigation: Goodwill is non-cash, amortized over 15 years under §197, and carries no cash obligation — its amortization is added back in the cash-flow build-up. SBA funding places approximately $1,576,854 of cash on the balance sheet at closing. The business is cash-generative on a normalized basis in both 2024 and 2025.

4. Execution Risk on the Ramp

Risk: The plan hires 17 writers in a new market inside one quarter and assumes $7,500 of revenue per writer per month.

Mitigation: The $7,500 / $4,500 assumption sits between two verified actual years (implied $7,162 in 2025; $9,235 in 2026 through August), and the condition-report department — the exact line the site replicates — runs at 43.6% against the 40% modeled. The $225,000 working-capital cushion covers the ($36,000) peak ramp draw 6.25× over. Manheim Dallas — $703,460 in 2025 — is the template being replicated.

5. Labor Model & Cost Inflation

Risk: Field labor is engaged as 1099 contractors; reclassification or a shift to W-2 would add an estimated 10–15% per writer in employer taxes and workers' compensation.

Mitigation: The per-production 1099 model means cost flexes with volume rather than sitting fixed. Rate-indexation clauses pass CPI and labor inflation through at renewal; the 2026 gross margin of 51.3% carries meaningful headroom against the 40% modeled.

6. Economic Downturn

Risk: Reduced auto sales and auction volume in a recession.

Mitigation: Reconditioning and condition reporting are essential to the used-vehicle market, which typically holds or gains share when new-vehicle sales soften. Variable cost structure limits downside operating leverage.

Conclusion

The $100,000 equity raise carries the Company to SBA close and rebuilds the non-anchor customer base; the SBA facility funds the expansion itself.
